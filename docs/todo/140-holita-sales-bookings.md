# 140 Book and manage sales appointments

**Status:** Ready after [135](135-holita-sales-agent-assignment.md), [139](139-holita-sales-appointment-services.md). **Product:** Holita.

## Background

red.bg offers a calendar and list, picks available slots from a booking service, and can link
a booking to a lead. Existing appointments keep the duration and buffer they were booked with.

## Goal

Let sales agents book, reschedule and close appointments from Sales or from a lead.

## Steps

1. Keep booking ID, platform tenant ID, service ID, optional same-tenant lead ID,
   status, notes, start/end/blocked-until instants, duration and buffer snapshots,
   creator and last editor Core user IDs, and created/updated timestamps. Keep Confirmed,
   Cancelled, Completed and No show.
2. Generate future slots from the active service's local schedule in Europe/Sofia,
   advancing by duration plus buffer. Revalidate at create, reschedule and reactivation,
   serializing the overlap check and write per affected service. A non-cancelled
   booking blocks its service through its buffer. Cancellation releases the slot,
   and service edits preserve existing booking snapshots.
3. Add Appointments calendar and list, detail, and a shared service/date/slot editor.
   Add URL-backed service, `status`, `scope=mine|team` and `period=today|next7Days`
   filters. Mine includes a booking once when the signed-in user created it or is
   currently assigned to its lead. Today is the current Sofia date. Next 7 days covers
   the following seven Sofia dates, starting tomorrow. Allow the dashboard to combine
   Mine, Confirmed and either period. Show linked appointments and a Book appointment
   action on Lead Detail.
4. Keep stable insert-only demo bookings on valid service slots (confirmed, completed
   and cancelled). Use a separate guarded verification schema and fix the server and
   browser reference time to Monday 2026-11-23 at 09:00 Europe/Sofia for date assertions.
   Configure an active weekday service, 09:00–17:00, with duration 45 and buffer 15.
   Book a confirmed slot Monday 10:00 created by the demo agent and Tuesday 11:00
   created by the other agent on a lead assigned to the demo agent. Add a confirmed
   other-only Monday 12:00 and a cancelled mine Monday 13:00. Keep the dated verification
   rows out of persistent development seeds.

## Done when

Two appointments cannot occupy overlapping time for a service, and cancelling a future
appointment frees its slot. Rescheduling updates the visible calendar and lead detail.
Agents can close an appointment as completed or no show. At the fixed reference time,
the demo agent's Mine + Confirmed list contains exactly Monday 10:00 for Today and
Tuesday 11:00 for Next 7 days. The other agent's booking and the cancelled booking
are absent from those results.

## Limits

Use a team schedule per service. No guest access, provider calendars, telephony, reminders
or meeting links generated by an external service.
