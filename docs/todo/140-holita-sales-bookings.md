# 140 Book and manage sales appointments

**Status:** Ready after [139](139-holita-sales-appointment-services.md). **Product:** Holita.

## Background

red.bg offers a calendar and list, picks available slots from a booking service, and can link
a booking to a lead. Existing appointments keep the duration and buffer they were booked with.

## Goal

Provide a tenant-scoped operation for booking eligible slots and editing their status.

## Steps

1. Keep booking ID, platform tenant ID, service ID, optional same-tenant lead ID,
   status, notes, start/end/blocked-until instants, duration and buffer snapshots,
   creator and last editor Core user IDs, and created/updated timestamps. Keep Confirmed,
   Cancelled, Completed and No show.
2. Generate future slots from the active service's local schedule in Europe/Sofia,
   advancing by duration plus buffer. Revalidate at create, reschedule and reactivation,
   serializing the overlap check and write per affected service. A non-cancelled
   booking blocks its service through its buffer. Cancellation releases the slot,
   and service edits preserve existing booking snapshots.
3. Expose scoped slot, booking detail and create, reschedule and status operations on the
   operator graph. Add stable insert-only confirmed, completed and cancelled demo bookings
   on valid slots. Leave the calendar and booking editor to 303.

## Done when

Through the Sales contract an agent can book or reschedule a valid slot and close a booking
as completed, no show or cancelled. An overlapping booking is rejected and cancelling a future
appointment frees its slot. Existing appointments retain their saved duration and buffer.

## Limits

Use a team schedule per service. No guest access, provider calendars, telephony, reminders
or meeting links generated by an external service.
