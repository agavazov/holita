### 1. Introduction to ERM Karma

Karma is a revolutionary, easy-to-use fleet management system, which offers a fresh solution for a real- time reporting system for drivers, vehicles, and roads.

The intuitive design allows Karma to function like an everyday application, which can be easily customized to the user's specific needs.

The Karma concept was developed based on ERM’s rich experience and deep understanding of the global fleet management, tracking, vehicle security and the telematics industry.

Karma is designed to provide immediate, real-time answers to fleet managers' and owners' questions.

### 1.1 Karma Functionality

With Karma, you can:

- Track your vehicles in real-time.

- Visually track your vehicles and focus on what you want to know.

- Simply ask your natural-language questions, and get detailed, visual answers in real-time.

- Generate detailed informative reports based on any of the Karma managed parameters, such as driver, vehicle, or events based on fuel, speed, driving behavior, temperature, and so on.

- Add POIs (points of interest) and plan the efficient route for a particular trip.

- Get notifications based on any of the Karma-managed parameters such as speed, drivers' routes, fuel status, driving behavior, geo fences, accidents events, location relative to points of interest, and so on.

- Have your drivers' behavior rated to better assess their performances.

- And much more...

### 4. Ask a Question

Karma incorporates a unique way to experience fleet management. It enables generating reports on- the-fly, as well as presenting a live visual presentation of the fleet/vehicle on a map for a simple query.

Answers for natural language queries such as: “show moving vehicles” or “cars with engine off” or “speed events over 120” are immediately displayed on the map.

With Karma, you can find specific answers to many commonly asked questions. By typing what you are looking for, in simple words, a detailed, visual answer is provided, including additional related information.

You can use a single parameter query, such as "David Smith location"; or multiple parameter queries, such as: "show all refuels today" or "driving behavior David Smith yesterday".

For more query-related question options:

1\. Click the ‘How to ask questions in the system?’ link at the Home Page, to view suggestions of relevant topics and questions that you can use.

### 7. Driving Behavior

Driving Behavior events include all abnormal maneuvers, such as turning while accelerating, aggressive braking, driving harshly on bumps, and so on. ### 12. Creating Notifications

### 12.1 Creating a New Notification
